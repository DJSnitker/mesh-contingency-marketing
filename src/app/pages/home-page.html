New in 1.0.0: weather alerts on the Public channel

Weather alerts. Over the mesh.

Mesh Contingency is a small bot for a MeshCore radio on a Linux computer. It watches the National Weather Service for the counties you list and posts each new alert to MeshCore’s Public channel, once, as it’s issued. Nobody joins anything or installs an app: every radio in range already has Public.

How it works

From the Weather Service to every radio

A computer with a MeshCore companion radio on USB runs the node. Every 10 minutes it asks api.weather.gov about your counties, and posts what’s new to the Public channel, where any radio in range reads it, even ones with no internet and no phone signal. An alert several counties share is posted once, and a restart never repeats one.
